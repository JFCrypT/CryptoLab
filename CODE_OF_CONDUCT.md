# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers must provide a respectful, professional, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in CryptoLab.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical and mathematical disagreements with evidence and precision.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect the project's educational scope and documented conventions.
- Avoid disclosing sensitive information or security reports publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unauthorized security
testing, and publication of private vulnerability information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy.
